# Annotation and formalization strategy — design history

## Version 1

Separate appellant, respondent, reporting justice, concurring/dissenting opinions and the final position. For each perspective, select passages identifying candidate causes and causal relations asserted, contested or rejected, including the reason for rejection.

Use a dedicated causal-unit table with ID, cause, outcome, relation, conclusion and status; a disagreement table recording reasons and effects on the conclusion; and a summary separating common from contested premises.

## Version 2

Retain perspective separation and explicitly record causal claims. A contestation attacks a causal claim, including one made by another perspective. Record legally relevant causes, the duty to act, rejected causes and reasons for rejection. Keep the causal-unit table, disagreement table and premise summary.

## Working example — prisoner harm

Candidate causes include negligent medical care, delayed care and inadequate cells. These are example reconstruction prompts, not measured conclusions about case 003.

| Perspective | Reconstruction prompts |
| --- | --- |
| Appellant | Position defended, including a claim for state payment due to delayed care; relevant facts, candidate causes and legally relevant cause |
| Respondent | Position defended, including adequate timing of care; relevant facts such as pre-existing conditions; rejected causes and facts countering the appellant; legally relevant cause |
| Reporting justice | Relevant facts accepted and rejected, reasons for deciding, legally relevant cause and duty to act; possible rhetorical weight or preference questions |
| Concurring opinions | Justice, conclusion, grounds, reasons for deciding and distinguishing points, such as conclusive versus inconclusive expert evidence |
| Dissenting opinions | Justice, conclusion, grounds, reasons for deciding, disagreements and whether majority arguments were addressed |
| STF — Final position | Causal relations recognized |

The [annotation protocol](protocol.md) contains the structured handoff specification. This document records the earlier design iterations.
